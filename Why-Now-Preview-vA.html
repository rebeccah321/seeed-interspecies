Technology for Interspecies Understanding

Life has been sending signals for 3.8 billion years.
We're only now learning to receive them.

In Mozambique, a bird called the honeyguide recruits humans to beehives. It calls, they follow, and they share the honey — a partnership between species that never learned each other's language, but developed a protocol anyway. Cross-species communication has always worked. It just never scaled.

01

Cross-species collaboration is nothing new.

Honeyguides lead hunters. Dolphins signal when to cast. Cormorants and hunting eagles have worked under human command for centuries. Homing pigeons carried addressed messages. Each was a real protocol — precise, effective, but hard to scale.

02

The signals were never the issue. The network was.

Calls, gaits, stripe patterns, water quality, temperature — animals and ecosystems have been broadcasting all of this for millions of years. What has always been missing is just the receiver: a shared network that connects them all.

03

Now the missing layer is finally here.

Edge AI, open-source hardware, and low-power mesh networks have crossed a threshold. For the first time, we can listen at scale — and those old handshakes can become a network.

04

We don't speak for animals. We build the tools that let them be heard.

Seeed is not a lab that speaks for animals. We build the open-source hardware that lets researchers and local teams turn those signals into useful knowledge — so any species, any ecosystem, can finally be heard.

What we do

Six Research Areas, One Platform

The six domains fill the anchors left by the receiver from the section above.

DOMAIN 01

Individual ID & lifecycle tracking

CV reads stripes and notches like barcodes — no capture needed.

DOMAIN 02

Population counting & dynamics

On-device AI counts; low-power mesh returns the numbers.

DOMAIN 03

Cognitive decline & aging

24/7 gait + vocal monitoring catches decline early.

DOMAIN 04

Acoustic monitoring

Edge sound ID with audio-visual cross-validation.

DOMAIN 05

Social behavior & group dynamics

Multi-camera arrays build social network graphs.

DOMAIN 06

Habitat & carrying capacity
