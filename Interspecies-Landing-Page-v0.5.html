Technology for
Interspecies Understanding

In Mozambique, a bird called the honeyguide does something remarkable. It flies to a person, calls, waits — then leads them, step by step, to a beehive. The human takes the honey and leaves the wax. Signaling, navigation, payment. A full collaboration protocol between two species — running for generations.

Cross-species communication has always been productive. It was just handcrafted, one relationship at a time. We're here to build the open tools — sensors, networks, AI — that let every species and every ecosystem finally be heard.

Why Now
🤝
Cross-species collaboration is nothing new.

The honeyguide leading humans to honey. Dolphins herd fish toward waiting fishermen in Brazil — a partnership passed down for generations.

📡
Signals are everywhere. We've been missing most of them.

A tiger's stripes are a barcode. Animals and ecosystems have been broadcasting data for millions of years.

⚡
Now, for the first time, we can listen at scale.

Edge AI, open-source hardware, and low-power mesh networks have crossed a threshold.

🔧
We build the tools that let them be heard.

Seeed is an open-hardware platform turning signals from any species into something any Fab Lab can replicate.
