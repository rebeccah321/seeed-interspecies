Understanding

Seeed Studio serves as the AI hardware and prototyping partner that turns research ideas into real-world systems. By combining edge AI computing, sensing, wireless connectivity, open-source hardware, and rapid manufacturing capabilities, Seeed can help researchers and communities move from experimental concepts to field-ready prototypes.

Whether that means detecting animal behavior, interpreting environmental signals, or enabling new forms of human–non-human interaction. Beyond prototyping, Seeed's manufacturing and global developer ecosystem can provide a pathway to validate, iterate, and scale successful projects into deployable products, bridging the gap between scientific exploration and technologies that can operate in the wild.

Traditional Pain Point

Mark-recapture & photo ID are invasive, expensive, labor-intensive. Camera trap images need manual review. Acoustic data requires expert listening. Behavioral coding is short-duration and subjective.

Seeed Open-Hardware Solution

Edge AI automates identification, counting, and coding. LoRa/Mesh enables long-range low-power data return. Open Datalogger middleware connects any sensor to any species question — no reinventing the wheel.

02

Technology Trends

The five AI capabilities reshaping what's possible at the edge — and how Seeed deploys them.

CV

Computer Vision

Species ID, individual recognition, counting, gait analysis — all on-device with reCamera.

TTS

Text-to-Speech

Generate species-appropriate audio cues, translate research signals into audible feedback.

LLM

Large Language Model

Interpret field data, generate research summaries, answer natural-language queries about observations.

VLM

Vision-Language Model

Multimodal: see an image and describe behavior, emotion, anomaly in natural language.

Tracker

GPS / LoRa Tracking

Individual movement, migration routes, proximity logging — low-power long-range mesh.

Wetland restoration validation

National-scale tiger monitoring

Bhutan needs nationwide tiger population monitoring. Traditional GPS collars have limited range and high cost. Mountain terrain has no cellular coverage. Need long-range, low-power, wide-area coverage.

BhutanTigerMesh

6 Project Categories

01
Wildlife Conservation

Understand, study, and protect wild animals. Camera traps, acoustics, collars, anti-poaching. The hardest, most rigorous space — the reputation anchor.

02
Human–Wildlife Conflict

Intrusion detection, real-time alerts, corridor monitoring. Resolve conflict proactively before damage occurs — not after.

03
Organisms as Sensors

Olfaction, acoustics, fungi, insects — biological systems as information inputs. Ultra-long-range sound capture, wind-noise removal.

04
Animal Husbandry & Smart Spaces

Livestock health, pet welfare × smart home automation. Animal behavior drives spatial response. Scale-up revenue engine candidate.

05
Interspecies Communication

Cross-species interaction tools — reButton with mic+speaker, vocal analysis, bidirectional sound. Enabling new forms of human–non-human interaction.

06
Zoo & Conservation Education

Controlled, easy to verify, has budget. Zoo multi-species monitoring + in-zoo FabLab for local manufacturing. The ideal second stop after conservation field.
